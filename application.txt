AI-Based Car Service Assistance Application – Draft

Application Type: Mobile Application
Backend: Python Django
Database: SQLite3
Frontend: Flutter
External Services: Mappls
AI Function: Vehicle issue detection from image + problem explanation

1. Project Overview

The AI Car Service Assistance Application helps vehicle owners quickly identify car problems and find nearby trusted service centers.

Users upload a photo of the car issue (for example engine leakage, tire damage, warning lights). The AI system analyzes the image and detects the possible problem.

After detecting the issue, the system automatically:

Explains the possible vehicle problem

Suggests urgency level

Searches nearby service centers from Google Maps

Filters the top 5 service centers based on ratings and reviews

Displays full details of those centers

Allows users to call the center directly

The application is designed to simplify vehicle troubleshooting and service center discovery.

Recommended service

6. Service Center Search Logic

After detecting the issue, the system determines the required service type.

Example mapping

Flat tire → Tire repair shop
Oil leakage → Engine service center
Brake issue → Brake specialist

Then Google Places API searches nearby service centers.

Filtering logic

Distance within 10 km
Rating above 3.5
Review count considered
Sort by rating and distance

Top 5 centers are returned.
